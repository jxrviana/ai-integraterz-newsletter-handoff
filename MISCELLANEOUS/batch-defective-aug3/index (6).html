The Short Version: Shopify says its partner ecosystem includes agencies, developers, and technology providers that help merchants build and grow. The point for an agency is not the size of the platform. It is the repeatable role the agency can own inside the customer journey.

The Details:

  • Pick one platform where your team already has proof.
  • Define the customer problem that precedes your service.
  • Create a handoff for the platform or partner team.

Why It Matters: A clear specialization makes an agency easier to recommend than a general promise.

The Short Version: AI partner programs are creating new routes for agencies that can implement tools rather than merely discuss them. The agency that gives partners a simple customer story will travel farther than the agency with the longest capability list.

The Details:

  • Name the workflow you improve.

  • Give the partner a sentence that avoids technical jargon.
