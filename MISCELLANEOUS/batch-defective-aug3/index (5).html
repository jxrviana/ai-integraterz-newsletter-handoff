COACHING NETWORKS

Certification bodies are widening the coaching conversation

Image placeholder: images/s1.jpg

The Short Version: Precision Nutrition presents coaching as a structured practice with education, behavior change, and client support at its center. That gives coaches a useful partnership question: which professionals serve the same client before or after the coaching need becomes obvious?

The Details:

  • Map adjacent professionals, not only competitors.
  • State what coaching does and does not cover.
  • Make the first conversation low pressure and specific.

Why It Matters: Trust grows when the referral explains the boundary as clearly as the benefit.

CLIENT HANDOFFS

Men’s health referrals need a careful next step

STORY IMAGE PLACEHOLDER · images/s2.jpg

Image placeholder: images/s2.jpg

The Short Version: NASM’s resource material covers wellness and nutrition coaching within a broader health and fitness context. A coach can use that context to design a handoff that supports the client without making medical promises.

The Details:

  • Use qualified partners for clinical questions.
  • Give the client one clear next action.
  • Report back only with appropriate consent.

Why It Matters: A responsible handoff protects both the client and the relationship that made the introduction.
