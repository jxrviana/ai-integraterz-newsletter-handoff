The Short Version: Sunrun’s partner and installer ecosystem shows how solar sits within a larger home-energy decision. For a local installer, the useful move is to build a small network around the moments when a homeowner is already reviewing the property.

The Details:

  • Choose partners who see the property early.
  • Explain qualification without overpromising savings.
  • Make the quote request easy to complete.

Why It Matters: The referral should reduce uncertainty, not add another sales pitch.

Incentive changes make local education more valuable

STORY IMAGE PLACEHOLDER · images/s2.jpg

Image placeholder: images/s2.jpg

The Short Version: Reporting on changes to federal solar incentives describes installers looking toward local programs and incentives. Because rules and eligibility can change, installers should use partners to route customers to current information rather than repeat stale claims.

The Details:

  • Keep incentive language current.
  • Train partners on what they can safely say.
  • Send technical questions to the installer.

Why It Matters: A trusted referral network is also an education network.
