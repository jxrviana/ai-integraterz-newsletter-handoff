Referrals Work Best When the Handoff Is Specific

STORY IMAGE PLACEHOLDER · images/s1.jpg

Image placeholder: images/s1.jpg

The Short Version: A referral is not a compliment. It is a handoff from one trusted context into another. The clearer the moment and the next step, the less work the partner has to do.

The Details:

  • Context: name the customer problem.
  • Language: give the partner a sentence.
  • Action: make the next step obvious.

Why It Matters: Specificity turns goodwill into movement.

The Best Partner Offer Starts With the Customer Journey

STORY IMAGE PLACEHOLDER · images/s2.jpg

Image placeholder: images/s2.jpg

The Short Version: Word of mouth sits inside the customer journey, not beside it. Map what happens before and after your service, then look for the trusted business that already owns the adjacent moment.

The Details:

  • Before: who sees the problem first?
  • During: who has the customer’s confidence?
  • After: what need appears next?

Why It Matters: The map tells you who to recruit before you write the offer.
