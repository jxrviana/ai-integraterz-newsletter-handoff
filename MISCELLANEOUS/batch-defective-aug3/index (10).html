THE PARTNER ECONOMY
 

BUYING NETWORKS

B2B buyers are bringing more people into the decision

STORY IMAGE PLACEHOLDER · images/s1.jpg

Image placeholder: images/s1.jpg

The Short Version: Forrester describes a shift from simple lead thinking toward buying groups and buying networks. The practical consequence is that a tech company should ask who else has the customer’s trust before it asks for a demo.

The Details:

  • Map the people around the problem.
  • Find the advisor who is trusted before the vendor search.
  • Give that advisor a clear reason to introduce you.

Why It Matters: Partner-led growth starts with the customer’s decision network, not the vendor’s CRM stages.

CHANNEL GROWTH

Partners are being treated as a growth engine

STORY IMAGE PLACEHOLDER · images/s2.jpg

Image placeholder: images/s2.jpg

The Short Version: Channelnomics frames partners as a larger part of how technology companies grow. That does not mean recruiting every possible partner. It means choosing the few relationships that make the customer’s implementation easier.

The Details:

  • Recruit for customer overlap.
  • Define the handoff by use case.
  • Measure partner activity and customer outcome separately.

Why It Matters: The right partner reduces buying friction because they already understand the environment.
