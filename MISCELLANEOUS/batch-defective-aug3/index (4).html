MEMBER ACQUISITION

The referral program is still one of the gym owner’s simplest growth plays

STORY IMAGE PLACEHOLDER · images/s1.jpg

Image placeholder: images/s1.jpg

The Short Version: Glofox describes referral programs as a practical way for gyms to turn member goodwill into new conversations. The useful lesson is operational: ask for a specific introduction, not a general recommendation.

The Details:

  • Give members a clear moment to refer.
  • Make the reward and next step easy to understand.
  • Track the source so the program can improve.

Why It Matters: The best gym referral is attached to a real customer moment, such as a friend asking where to train.

HEALTH PARTNERSHIPS

Gyms are becoming part of a wider health network

STORY IMAGE PLACEHOLDER · images/s2.jpg

Image placeholder: images/s2.jpg

The Short Version: The Health & Fitness Association’s partnership work points to a wider role for fitness businesses in local health ecosystems. That creates room for relationships with clinicians, therapists, employers, and community organizations.

The Details:

  • Look beyond other gyms for partners.
  • Choose partners whose customers already have a relevant need.
  • Build a handoff that respects the customer’s context.

Why It Matters: A gym can grow through trusted adjacency, not only through another advertisement.
