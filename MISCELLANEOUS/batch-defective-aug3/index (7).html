AGENCY NETWORKS

PR collectives turn geographic coverage into a client benefit

STORY IMAGE PLACEHOLDER · images/s1.jpg

Image placeholder: images/s1.jpg

The Short Version: Enterie’s expansion describes a collective model that gives clients access to PR capability across more markets. For smaller agencies, the lesson is practical: a trusted specialist network can make a referral more useful than a generic “we know someone” introduction.

The Details:

  • Define the problem your agency owns.
  • List the markets or specialties you can cover through partners.
  • Make the receiving agency part of the promise.

Why It Matters: A referral network becomes credible when the client can understand why each participant is involved.

AI PARTNERSHIPS

AI services are creating new PR moments for agencies

STORY IMAGE PLACEHOLDER · images/s2.jpg

Image placeholder: images/s2.jpg

The Short Version: The reported Brandi AI agency partnership involving Hinge Marketing shows agencies entering partner ecosystems around AI services. That creates a referral opening for PR firms that can explain the human, market, and reputation side of an AI rollout.

The Details:

  • Partner around a defined launch or risk moment.
  • Give the technology partner a clear PR trigger.
  • Keep the recommendation grounded in the client’s situation.

Why It Matters: The strongest PR referral is attached to a decision the client already has to make.
