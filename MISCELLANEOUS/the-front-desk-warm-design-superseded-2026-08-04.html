MEMBERSHIP DATA

US fitness industry extends record visitation streak through 2025

The Short Version: The industry ended 2025 with a 19-quarter streak of foot-traffic growth, according to HFA data summarized by the association.

The Details:

  • 184,000+ visits per location in 2025, up 4.2% year over year.
  • The dataset covers roughly 11,000 facilities across the market.
  • All four facility segments grew during the year.
  • Consumers are projected to spend $60 billion on fitness in 2026.

Why It Matters: A fuller market is not permission to coast. It raises the value of retention, scheduling, and a front desk that can convert interest into a visit.

MEMBERSHIP DATA

The US now has 81 million fitness-facility members

The Short Version: HFA's 2026 consumer report says membership and facility use reached record levels, while churn and non-use moved in the opposite direction.

The Details:

  • 81 million Americans held a fitness-facility membership.
  • Membership penetration reached 26.1% among Americans age 6 and older.
  • More than 100 million people used a fitness facility in 2025.
  • The report is based on a survey of 18,000 US residents.

Why It Matters: The next operating question is not whether demand exists. It is whether your team makes joining, returning, and asking for help feel easy.

MEMBER ACQUISITION

The referral channel is an operating system, not a favor

The Short Version: Most gyms already receive informal introductions. The missing piece is a repeatable handoff: who makes the introduction, what the prospect sees next, and how the gym closes the loop.

The Details:

  • A referral needs a named owner at the gym.
  • The introduction should carry context, not just a phone number.
  • The prospect needs one clear next step.
  • The referring member deserves a visible, timely follow-up.

Why It Matters: For operators, an affiliate program can turn trusted member and partner relationships into a measurable acquisition channel without making the member experience feel like a pitch.

WORKFORCE

Fitness hiring has a readiness gap, not just a headcount gap

The Short Version: ISSA's 2026 Fitness Hiring Report says operators need trainers who can contribute to retention and business operations, not only deliver technically sound sessions.

The Details:

  • US fitness-trainer employment is projected to grow 12% from 2024 to 2034.
  • The report estimates roughly 74,200 domestic openings annually.
  • Snap Fitness is seeking to add 4,000 trainers globally.
  • Anytime Fitness reported a shortfall of about 1,300 coaches across 2,300 domestic locations.

Why It Matters: A hiring plan that stops at certification leaves the most expensive part to chance: whether a new coach can build trust, retain clients, and support the business.
