## Palette
- Page background `#f4f4f5`; card background `#ffffff`
- Card border `1px solid #e4e4e7`, border-radius `10px`, padding `24px`, `16px` gap between cards
- Near-black (masthead, section bars, footer) `#0b0b0c`
- Body text `#27272a`; muted `#71717a`
- Category label `#8a8a8f`, UPPERCASE, letter-spacing `1.5px`, `11px`, bold
- Headlines & links `#2563a8` (medium blue), bold; links underlined
- Bold lead-ins ("The short version:", "The details:", "Why it matters:") in `#111` bold
- Footer accent links `#e0a83a` (gold); social bar `#2f6fb0`

## Type
`Arial, 'Helvetica Neue', Helvetica, sans-serif`. Body `15px`/line-height `1.65`. Headline links `19px` bold. Labels `11px`. Section bars `13px` bold uppercase.
