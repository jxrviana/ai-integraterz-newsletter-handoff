# SPEC 3 — BOLD v2 (PREMIUM & MODERN)

This is NOT a clone — it's an original, premium, modern design with a confident bold identity, taking cues from the clean editorial polish of The Rundown and The Future Today (SPEC 1 & 2) but with its own strong accent. Read SHARED.md for rules, branding, and content. Save to:
`C:\Users\Xander\Claude\Cowork\AI Integraterz\Newsletter\design-directions\v2\html-3-bold.html`

Vibe: a high-end modern brand — think a premium tech publication. Confident and clean, NOT loud or busy. Big headlines, disciplined whitespace, ONE accent used sparingly. NO emojis, no gradients, no slop.

## Palette
- Page background `#ffffff`; card background `#ffffff`
- Card border `1px solid #ececec`, border-radius `4px` (sharp, modern), padding `28px`
- Masthead / footer near-black `#111214`
- Accent ORANGE `#ff5a1f` — used SPARINGLY: category labels, a thin top rule, the CTA button, small marks. NEVER color the headlines with it.
- Headlines near-black `#14161a`, bold, LARGE `28px`, tight leading
- Body `#33353a`; muted `#8a8d93`; bold lead-ins `#14161a`

## Type
`Arial, 'Helvetica Neue', Helvetica, sans-serif`, heavier weights for headlines. Body `15px`/`1.6`. **Headlines `28px` bold near-black.** Category labels `11px` ORANGE UPPERCASE letter-spacing `2px` bold.

## Layout (600px centered)
1. **Top:** a solid `4px` orange bar across the very top; below it a small right-aligned `Read online` link.
2. **Masthead:** near-black block, refined (not chunky). Left-aligned white wordmark **THE DISPATCH** (`24px` bold) with a small orange square accent mark; a thin orange rule under it; small grey tagline `The one-line promise your newsletter makes.`
3. **Intro:** `Good morning.` bold + greeting; then the second intro line set as a callout with a `3px` orange left rule.
4. **In today's issue:** clean list with small orange square bullets (5 TOC items).
5. **Divider:** a thin `#ececec` rule with a small orange caps label `LATEST` sitting on it.
6. **Story cards (3; card 3 is the HOUSE/NATIVE, byte-identical style):** white card, thin border, generous padding. Each: orange caps category label → LARGE near-black headline (NO emoji) → flat grey image block → `The short version:` bold + lead → disc bullets → `Why it matters:` bold + text → a chunky orange `READ MORE` button (uppercase, `12px`, `#ff5a1f`, white text, radius `4px`). Cards A (TECHNOLOGY), B (BUSINESS), C (THE DISPATCH, native).
7. **Utility:** `One free thing` — a near-black panel (`#111214`, white text) with the heading and the prompt copy; premium and quiet.
8. **Ratings:** minimal — one line `What did you think of this issue?` and three small bordered inline options: `Nailed it`, `Solid`, `Needs work`.
9. **Sign-off:** `See you soon,` + `— The Editor, THE DISPATCH`.
10. **Footer:** near-black block, refined, thin orange top rule; `Update your email preferences or unsubscribe here.` / `© 2026 AI Integraterz` / `37460 Beacon Brick Road, Zephyrhills, Florida 33541`.

Premium, modern, confident, restrained. Big clean headlines, one disciplined orange accent, lots of whitespace. NO emojis anywhere.
