# General Skill — Newsletter Types (Justin's taxonomy)

Justin defined four newsletter TYPES at the Aug 4 huddle. Each newsletter is one type, and the type drives the format + voice. (This is why there's no single universal template — a story newsletter and a news newsletter are shaped differently.)

## The four types
1. **Editorial / informational** — the "standard" trade read (reference: **MyClaw**). News + genuine value, professionally reported. Our niche/industry newsletters live here (pure education), and so does The Dealmaker (Brian Kurtz style).
2. **Editorial / independent news** — a point-of-view news channel (reference: **Substack — Zeteo, Breaking Points**; YouTube-news energy). Opinionated commentary on a beat, a strong lead + dispatches. The Referral Channel lives here.
3. **Storytelling** — one continuous narrative that sells (reference: **Bill Mueller / Story Sales Machine**). Main Street Affiliate, and the story spine of Local Affiliate Pro / The Open Source.
4. **Promotional** — openly promotional, story-led (reference: **Sendivo**). Local Affiliate Pro sits at story/promotional (StoryBrand).

## Which newsletter is which
| Newsletter | Type | Reference / soul |
|---|---|---|
| **The Dealmaker** | editorial / informational | Brian Kurtz; Jay Abraham "preeminence" |
| **The Referral Channel** | editorial / independent news | Substack / Breaking Points |
| **Local Affiliate Pro** | story / promotional | StoryBrand — local businesses take back their power |
| **The Open Source** | editorial / informational | Donald Miller storytelling; anti-big-AI superhero theme |
| **Main Street Affiliate** | storytelling | Story Sales Machine (Beehiiv opt-in flagship) |
| **Niche/industry** (Front Desk, Protocol, Green Room, People Person, Daily Sun, Agency Insider) | editorial / informational | pure education, build trust — NO offer |

## Two rules that follow from the types
- **Niche/industry newsletters = pure education.** No native affiliate card, no bottom CTA, no offer. Their job is trust + sender stats; conversion happens elsewhere.
- **Flagships (Dealmaker, Referral) + Local Affiliate Pro keep a conversion path** — mission-framed ("see what your network is building"), not a hard pitch.
