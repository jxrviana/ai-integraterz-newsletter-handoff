## Voice & persona
Write as a **seasoned editorial / direct-response mind** — think a Brian-Kurtz-type veteran who has "sent millions of pieces of mail" and knows what actually moves people. Traits:
- **Authoritative and teaching**, never hypey. Earns trust by being useful first.
- **Deal-savvy** — talks in terms of relationships, leverage, reciprocity, and the long game, not funnels and hacks.
- **Plain, confident, senior.** Short declaratives. One idea at a time. Respects the reader's intelligence.
- Channels **Jay Abraham's** worldview: fall in love with your client, not your product; serve before you sell; you are your client's most trusted advisor.
- **No banned words** (game-changing, unlock, revolutionize, imagine if, supercharge) and none of that register generally — the voice is the opposite of that.

## Format — editorial letter / column (its own structure)
The Dealmaker presents as a **single authored editorial column**, not a 6-card news roundup and not a deals roundup. Keep the Issue 1 skeleton, re-pointed to teaching:
1. **Masthead** — logo + wordmark + tagline + `No. 00X · Month Year · Johana Buitrago`.
2. **Banner image** (non-canned — see The Look).
3. **"Good morning."** open — one warm, senior paragraph that frames today's principle.
4. **Thesis pull-quote** — the preeminence idea in one line.
5. **3–5 labeled editorial sections** (e.g. *The Principle · The Move · Why It Pays · One Real Play*) — bolds/italics, one image-as-format per section or two, a **quote block** for an Abraham idea or an expert line. Use the **Short Version / The Details / Why It Matters** device inside a section where it earns its place.
6. **One practical "steal this" box** — the week's concrete move (the Issue 1 "This Monday" box worked; keep that pattern).
7. **One CTA block** — editorial invitation → `https://whimsy-nebula-47ch.here.now/`, plus the reply option.
8. **Footer** — compliant (below).

Mention discipline: keep the offer light-touch — the value teaches, the CTA closes. Roughly the Issue 1 cadence (one CTA block + one reply prompt), not a pitch in every section.
