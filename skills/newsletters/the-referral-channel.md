## The new direction (read first)

Every AII newsletter is now its own **publication**, not an email off a shared template. A reader who gets two of ours the same day must **never** suspect one sender. So **fonts, colors, logo, images, layout, structure, and topics all differ per title** — zero shared footprint. The one thing we keep is the **formatting craft** Justin praised (warm human intro, bold/italics, text blocks, "Short Version / The Details / Why It Matters," quote blocks, image-as-format). We change the **look and identity**, not the craft.

**Justin's #1 ask for this title:** make The Referral Channel look **completely different from The Dealmaker** — different formatting, logo, images, colors, layout. The old images read "vintage / canned / same-source" and too close to the Dealmaker. This title needs its own distinct visual world.

**Universal design changes (all titles):** WHITE background · WIDER layout · its own logo · its own image style. Quality over quantity — keep it simple.

## Visual identity — must NOT resemble The Dealmaker

Design the title as an **independent news channel** ("Channel" = broadcast). Concrete direction:

**Logo / masthead.** A **wordmark**, not a badge or seal — "THE REFERRAL CHANNEL" in a bold condensed news face, treated as a **broadcast lower-third / chyron bar** (accent-color bar, wordmark reversed out white) across the top. Optional small broadcast glyph — a "live" dot, a signal arc, or a ▸. Flat, modern, high-contrast. This alone should read as a different publication from the Dealmaker at a glance.

**Color.** White background (universal). Signature = **newsroom ink-black + one electric editorial accent.** Default accent: a confident **broadcast red** for the chyron bar, rules, links, and category labels. **Guardrail:** the accent must not mirror the Dealmaker's palette — if the Dealmaker lands on a warm red/burgundy, switch this title's accent to an **electric cobalt** instead. Two accent colors max, plus black and white. Keep it clean.

**Typography (email-safe reality).** The look is **condensed, caps-y, news-desk headlines + a readable body.** In email you cannot rely on custom web fonts — they silently fall back and the differentiation is lost — so carry the identity with **email-safe stacks + treatment**, not a font download: headlines in bold Arial/Helvetica set **ALL CAPS with tight letter-spacing** (chyron feel), or a heavy Georgia for an op-ed feel; body in a clean serif/sans for the commentary read. The difference from the Dealmaker comes from **caps, weight, tracking, hairline rules, the chyron bar, color, and layout** — not from a special font.

**Image style.** Pick ONE distinct lane and hold it for consistency: **modern editorial photojournalism** — real, current, documentary deal-making / business scenes, natural light, journalistic framing — **or** bold flat editorial illustration in the accent color. **Avoid:** sepia/vintage filters, generic stock "handshake" clichés, and anything that shares a source or model with the Dealmaker. Add a news cue — a thin accent rule or a small caption bar under each image. **Generate on a different tool/model than the Dealmaker** to kill the same-source feel. *(Xander generates all images from text-safe prompts — this skill supplies style + prompt direction only; Claude never generates images.)*

**Layout.** **Wider** than the old narrow centered column. Independent-news rhythm — a strong lead plus dispatches — not a grid of equal cards.

## Format & structure (independent-news, not dry cards)

Keep the craft; change the shape. Suggested issue architecture:

1. **Masthead / chyron** — logo bar, tagline ("Independent coverage of the referral economy"), issue no. + date, `Read Online` | `Unsubscribe`.
2. **Anchor's note** — the warm human open, reframed as Jay Bell's cold-open: one POV line that frames the issue (*Breaking Points* "monologue" energy).
3. **The Lead** — one substantial, opinionated analysis of a current development. This is the centerpiece: independent news leads with a strong take, not six equal items. Full craft here — bold/italics, a pull-quote block, an image-as-format, and an editorial take on "Short Version / The Details / Why It Matters."
4. **Dispatches** — 2–4 shorter opinionated segments from the beat (not dry cards; each carries a POV).
5. **The native segment (the offer)** — ONE segment styled **byte-identical** to the surrounding commentary, mission-framed ("see what operators in your network are building") → CTA. Must be indistinguishable from editorial; verify parity programmatically (general playbook), never by eye.
6. **The Playbook** — a teach-something-free utility segment (keep the free-value craft).
7. **Sign-off** — named Jay Bell sign-off + one mission-framed CTA line (2nd and final touch).
8. **Footer** — compliant (below).

**Mention budget: exactly 2** — the native segment + the sign-off line. Quality over quantity.
