## Type
**Story / promotional — StoryBrand** (Donald Miller). One continuous narrative per issue — storytelling that sells. It sits at the intersection of the **promotional** lane (Sendivo) and the **storytelling** lane (Story Sales Machine / StoryBrand). It openly promotes — like The Dealmaker, it builds to **one clear ask** — but it does it through a story with the **reader cast as the hero**. This is NOT a disguised-ask editorial (that's the niches) and NOT a roundup of cards.

## Mission (the soul of it)
Give local **"Main Street"** businesses — the barbershop, the solar installer, the HVAC guy, the home-services crew — the same **"unfair advantage"** the big brands have always had, through affiliate marketing. And show them they can **take back their power** using the one thing Main Street already owns and big brands can only fake: **networking + community — the local business advantage.**

## Theme — BREAKING FREE
Every issue carries the same spine: **breaking free.** Free of the advertising treadmill. Free of "Big Brother" — the Facebook-ad / Google-ad pay-to-play machine that quietly took Main Street hostage. Free of the stuff local businesses were *told* they had to do — and back to what made local business *local* in the first place: neighbors, word of mouth, community, trust. Hopeful and empowering, **never doom-y or conspiratorial.** The reader should finish feeling like the game was rigged, they just found the cheat code — and it was theirs all along.

## Topics / angles (the "news" that carries the story)
Each issue hangs the mission on one real, current angle:
- How local businesses are **taking back their power.**
- How companies are **breaking free of the advertising game** (leaving the ad platforms, owning their own audience).
- How local businesses use **affiliate marketing — and other moves — to make more money.**

Pick one true angle, tell it as a story, land the mission.

## StoryBrand framing (non-negotiable)
- **The local business owner is the HERO.** Not us. Never us.
- **We are the GUIDE** — empathy ("you've been squeezed, and it isn't your fault") + authority ("here's the path, and here's proof it works"). We hand the hero the unfair advantage; *they* win it.
- **The plan:** affiliate marketing + community/networking = the way out.
- **The stakes:** stay dependent on rented ad platforms and keep losing to big brands (failure) → own your audience and your network and out-*local* the giants (success).
- **The CTA shifts** from "let us help you" → **"see what your community / network is building."** Invite them into a movement; don't pitch them a service.

## Format — one story, start to finish
Keep the **formatting craft** Justin loves (it carries across all our newsletters) — just not the news-card scaffold:
- ✅ Warm **"good-morning" intro** · confident **bolds/italics** · clean **text blocks** · **pull-quotes / quote blocks** · **image-as-format** (an image used as a design beat, not decoration).
- ❌ **No** "Short Version / The Details / Why It Matters" cards · ❌ **no** table-of-contents · ❌ **no** numbered offer list. Those are the editorial/roundup formats — LAP is a single narrative.

Per-issue shape (the StoryBrand arc):
1. **Masthead** — Local Affiliate Pro logo + a mission-flavored tagline + `Issue 00X · Month Year`.
2. **Warm open** — greet the reader as a fellow Main-Streeter; name the feeling (squeezed, out-spent, tired of feeding the ad machine).
3. **The story / the angle** — the real, current hook, told as narrative. The hero is a local business (or the reader themselves) breaking free.
4. **The turn** — reveal the unfair advantage: affiliate marketing + community/networking. Empathy, then authority. Proof where we have it.
5. **The plan** — what "taking back your power" actually looks like, in plain steps.
6. **One clear CTA** — "see what your community / network is building." The story earns the ask; the ask is direct and singular (one CTA, like The Dealmaker).
7. **Footer** — compliant (below).

## Voice
Warm, grounded, on the reader's side. Champion-of-the-underdog energy without the conspiracy hat. Concrete over abstract — a real barbershop, a real solar crew, not "SMBs." Empower, don't lecture. The reader is the hero of every sentence.

## Design — its OWN distinct look (the shared template is retired)
**Universal (all newsletters, per Aug 4):** **white background** · **wider layout** (drop the narrow ~600px centered column) · **its own logo** · **its own images** (no canned/vintage/same-source stock — a distinct feel per newsletter) · quality over quantity, keep it simple.

**LAP-specific:** a **warm, local, community feeling** — Main Street, not Madison Avenue. Warm neutral palette on white with a single grounded accent (workshop / kraft / civic warmth, not corporate blue). Imagery = real local storefronts, tradespeople, neighbors, a handshake on a real street — human and specific, never stocky. LAP must read as a **visibly different publication** from The Dealmaker, The Referral Channel, and the niches: a reader who receives two of ours the same day should never guess they share a sender.

## Compliance & build (unchanged)
- **No "AI Integraterz" / "Published by AI Integraterz"** anywhere. **Never "power partner"** (→ "the affiliate program"). No banned words (game-changing, unlock, revolutionize, imagine if, supercharge). No false relationship claims (treat as cold outbound until/unless the destination is opt-in).
- **No fabricated facts, ever.** Every stat traces to a real fetched source; any named real business must be real and browser-verifiable. If you use a composite/illustrative hero, **frame it clearly as illustrative** — never present an invented business as a real named case.
- **Email-safe HTML:** inline all CSS (no `<head><style>` block — clients strip it), ASCII/HTML entities only, merge tags `{{ MessageURL }}` / `{{ UnsubscribeURL }}`.
- **Footer:** `Local Affiliate Pro` + `© 2026 Local Affiliate Pro` + physical address `37460 Beacon Brick Road, Zephyrhills, Florida 33541` + Unsubscribe + View online. **No social links. No signup link** (until the destination decides otherwise).
- **Images:** Xander generates them from text-safe prompts — **SingleBrain never generates images.** No Medium article for this newsletter (that's the editorial format).
- **Draft only; never auto-send; Xander approves before any send.**
