# Newsletter Skills Library

The shared source of truth for creating AI Integraterz outbound newsletters. **Both Claude and SingleBrain build from these** — keep them identical and updated as standards change.

> **★ CURRENT DIRECTION (Aug 4–5 2026 huddle — full capture in `../MEETING-TAKEAWAYS-2026-08-04.md`).**
> **Every newsletter is its own DISTINCT PUBLICATION** — its own persona, mission, and look (fonts, colors, logo, images, layout, topics). No shared "footprint": a reader who gets two of ours in a day must never guess they share a sender. **Keep the formatting craft** (good-morning intro, bolds, text blocks, Short Version/Details/Why It Matters, quote blocks) — change the LOOK + identity per newsletter. Universal: **white background, wider layout.** The old "one locked Front Desk template for all niches" is **RETIRED.**
> **Niche/industry newsletters are now PURE EDUCATION** — no advert, no native card, no bottom CTA (trust-builders only). **Flagships (Dealmaker, Referral) + Local Affiliate Pro keep a mission-framed conversion path.**
