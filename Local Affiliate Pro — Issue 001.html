LOCAL AFFILIATE PRO
A practical shortlist of offers local businesses can put to work.
Issue 001 · August 2026
Edited by Jordan Reyes

This is a running shortlist of affiliate offers worth a closer look, curated for local businesses and the audiences they already serve. Each issue focuses on programs that can create a useful recommendation, a new service line, or a more valuable customer relationship.

01

Shopify Affiliate Program

Shopify pays affiliates for referring new merchants and supports creators, publishers, commerce experts, and app partners.

Local angle: Web studios, chambers, makers, and local business educators can recommend a storefront path to merchants moving from in-person sales into online commerce.

02

HubSpot Affiliate Program

HubSpot offers CRM, marketing, sales, and customer-service software, with 30% recurring commission for up to one year.

Local angle: Consultants and agencies can introduce a recognizable system for turning scattered leads, follow-ups, and customer conversations into one operating view.

03

GetResponse Affiliate Program

GetResponse is an email marketing and automation platform with affiliate commissions of up to 60% per referral.

Local angle: Local marketers can pair the offer with newsletters, appointment reminders, lead magnets, and follow-up campaigns that businesses can own.

04

Speaker Agent

An AI-powered platform that connects experts directly with podcast hosts and show producers.

Local angle: PR firms, coaches, consultants, and local experts can recommend a path to credible podcast exposure without a bureau or cold-outreach workload.

05

FreshBooks Affiliate Program

FreshBooks offers trackable referral links, approved resources, and performance insights through PartnerStack for its accounting software.

Local angle: Accountants, bookkeepers, and small-business communities can recommend a simpler invoicing and expense workflow to service businesses.

06

ActiveCampaign Affiliate Program

ActiveCampaign combines email marketing, marketing automation, and CRM, with a 30% recurring commission for up to 12 months.

Local angle: A strong fit for agencies and consultants helping local operators turn inquiries into timely, personalized follow-up.

07

The Affiliate Machine

A done-for-you digital marketing course and master-resell-rights opportunity.

Local angle: Training businesses and community operators can point entrepreneurial audiences toward a ready-made online-business model with a clear product to study.

08

Kinsta Hosting Affiliate Program

Kinsta offers managed WordPress hosting with a one-time bonus of up to $500 plus lifetime monthly commissions of 10%.

Local angle: Web designers and developers can recommend hosting alongside a site build, migration, or care plan.

09

ConnectMed

A turnkey wellness platform for clinics, med spas, gyms, and fitness businesses, with access to 50+ wellness products and a licensed provider network.

Local angle: Wellness operators can add a structured care and revenue conversation without taking on provider, compliance, or fulfillment work themselves.

10

monday.com Affiliate Program

monday.com gives affiliates marketing resources and says referrals can earn up to 100% commission on the first year's sales.

Local angle: Operations consultants can give growing local teams a visible way to coordinate projects, sales, service, and internal work.

11

Hostinger Affiliate Program

Hostinger's affiliate program is free to join and includes tracking, affiliate banners, and performance-based commissions.

Local angle: Freelancers, educators, and community groups can pair a hosting recommendation with domain, website, or small-business launch support.
