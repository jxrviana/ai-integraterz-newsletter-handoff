One Real Play
Steal this: the Partner Pact.

You don’t need us to start. Affiliate expert Amber Spears teaches a move called the Power of Eight: pick a handful of businesses that serve your exact customer but don’t compete with you — the med spa’s dermatologist, the roofer’s solar installer, the CPA’s bookkeeper — and agree to actively send each other business. Pay them what a customer already costs you to acquire, but only after the sale closes. It costs nothing you weren’t already spending.

This Monday: open your last 20 customers. Write down every non-competing business they already pay. Pick three you’d happily send a customer to, and message each proposing one reciprocal intro this month — with a commission on any closed sale. That’s the seed of a real program.

(It works for three partners. It falls apart at fifteen — no tracking, no agreements, nobody following up. That’s exactly where we come in.)
