The Morning Routine Myth

We're often told that to be successful, we need to wake up at 5 AM, meditate for an hour, journal, and run a 10K before breakfast. But forcing a routine that doesn't fit your natural rhythm creates stress, not peace.

This week, try focusing on just one small, intentional act each morning. It could be savoring your coffee without your phone, stretching for five minutes, or simply taking three deep breaths before getting out of bed.

5 Minutes of Stillness

If you're feeling overwhelmed, pause. You don't need an hour of meditation to reset your nervous system. Try this simple 5-minute grounding exercise:

1. Find a comfortable seat and close your eyes.
2. Notice three sounds you can hear.
3. Notice two things you can physically feel (e.g., your feet on the floor).
4. Take one deep, intentional breath.
