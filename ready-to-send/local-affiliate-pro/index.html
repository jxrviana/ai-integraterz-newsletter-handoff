The Main Street Advantage

You do the work, you know the customers, you’ve earned the trust — yet every month another slice of your margin goes to renting attention from a machine built for brands with bigger budgets. That squeeze is real. It’s also not the whole story.

The advantage was never the ad budget

Jobber — the field-service software many home-service pros already run on — offers an affiliate program built for exactly this crowd. Its page invites people with “an audience of field service business owners” to refer small businesses through a unique tracking link and earn revenue. Proof the affiliate channel is wide open to the home-service community, not just internet celebrities. (A real example, not a sponsor.)
The three-step way out

Start with a real need — recommend only what you’d stand behind without a payout. Map the neighbors — find three businesses whose customers overlap with yours but whose work doesn’t. Track what compounds — use a legitimate affiliate link, disclose it plainly, and keep your own record of introductions and repeat business.
Big brands buy reach. Main Street builds belonging.

That’s the movement we’re building: local operators trading trust, useful tools, and introductions until the old rules stop being the only rules. Your customers know who to call. Your neighbors know who to recommend. Your network becomes an asset that grows while you serve people.
