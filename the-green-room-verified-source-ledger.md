## STORY CARD 4: Can Specialized PR Firms Survive the Consolidation Wave?

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Final URL** | https://www.prdaily.com/can-specialized-firms-survive-industry-consolidation/ |
| **HTTP Status** | 200 |
| **Publication** | PR Daily |
| **Published** | February 27, 2025 |
| **Title** | Can specialized firms survive industry consolidation? |

### Exact Supporting Quotes (independently verified August 3, 2026):
1. "The global communications industry experienced a seismic shift at the end of 2024 as three of the world's largest agencies, Edelman, Omnicom and IPG, implemented large-scale organizational changes focused on financial and operational efficiency."
2. "Operating one of those boutique-style brands requires separate staffing, additional overhead and everything that goes with standing up a separate brand, including the cost of a separate CEO," said Kim Sample, president of the PR Council.
3. "I think the boutique model is sustainable because you have a need for specialists, you have a need for generalists," said Jacqueline Babb, a senior lecturer at Northwestern University. "The boutique firm, if it's by itself, gives you the specialists; if it's within a broader firm, (it) gives you specialties and generalists. I don't think that need goes away."
4. "Clients really want that integrated approach, and they don't want to have multiple agencies they need to coordinate," said Annie Pace Scranton, founder and CEO of Pace Public Relations.
5. "Clients really appreciate senior talent," Sample said. "But they're asking for more while expecting lower costs. That creates a lot of pressure on firms of all sizes."
6. "In this age where we're having to invest a lot in technology, generative AI being a big piece of it, it's hard to justify all these different costs unless you've got a very clear reason for doing so," Sample added.
7. "Because digital marketing has provided so much direct attribution for us, we're trying to seek it in other areas as well," Babb said. "PR firms are starting to see the effects of that, and so they are having to show ROI in a more meaningful way."
