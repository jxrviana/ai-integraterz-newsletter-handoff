## 6. SPLIT-TEST PHILOSOPHY — "test twice as much as you send"

- **Control + 2–3 variants.** Send the same list a **control** and variants, compare.
- **Same list, different times.** Main version at 10am; a variant at 2pm or 5–6pm. Then **swap the send times** the next day (what won at 2pm, try at 6pm). Four hours can swing opens/clicks massively.
- **Designed vs plain.** Build a **super-plain, text-style variant** (Sendiva / "paid ads vs affiliate" style) of the direct newsletter and send it to the same list at a different time — test plain-vs-designed.
- **Editorial vs direct.** Referral Channel (editorial) vs The Dealmaker (direct) is itself an ongoing split-test.
- **Put the results on LinkedIn.** "This email got 200 clicks / converted 10% / booked X calls" → that's the content that pulls clients in.
