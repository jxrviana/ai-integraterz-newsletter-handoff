The four questions everyone asks

“I've tried a VA before and it was more work than it saved.”

Almost everyone has, and it's usually true. A general assistant arrives with no context, so you become their trainer and their manager. Ours arrive trained on recruitment process, already inside our systems, and we manage them. The difference isn't the person — it's everything wrapped around them.

“How do I know they'll be any good?”

We recruit and vet against a recruitment-specific bar, train them before they're assigned, and replace them if the fit is wrong. You never run a hiring process and never have to let anyone go.

“What if my process is different?”

It will be. The first week is spent learning your desk — your ATS, your templates, your tone, your definition of a good candidate. They work your way, not a generic way.

“What happens to my data and my candidates?”

They work inside your systems, under your access controls, and everything they build stays in your ATS. You own all of it.
