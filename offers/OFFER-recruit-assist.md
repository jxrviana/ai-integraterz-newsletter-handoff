## What it is (one line)
A **trained, managed recruitment assistant placed on a recruiter's desk** — running outreach, sourcing, or both — for a flat monthly fee, with none of the employment risk of a hire. Tagline: *"Split the desk, keep the billings."*

## Who it's for
Independent recruitment, staffing & search firms **~1–20 heads**, where the owner/billers still do their own BD + sourcing — the **People Person** niche. The classic 360 biller stuck in the BD-vs-delivery whipsaw.

## Category & guarantee (canonical — important)
Recruit Assist is a **CAPACITY offer, not a go-to-market one — so it carries NO pipeline guarantee.** The $50k / 90-day pipeline guarantee lives on the **separate** offer, *The Unreplaceable Recruiter* (`/unreplaceable-expert`). Delivery uses the **same embedded model as our Embedded Engineer offer** — Recruit Assist is, in Justin's words, *"the Embedded Engineer, for recruiters."* The included systems = **"The Single Brain"** (the full tool/agent stack we've built, not one seat; nothing to license on top).

## The problem it solves
A 360 desk has two full-time jobs (win the work + fill the work) and one person; whichever is loudest that week wins, so the pipeline empties every time delivery gets busy. Recruit Assist puts another pair of hands on one (or both) sides of the desk.

## What the assistant does — 4 pillars
1. **Outreach** — LinkedIn campaigns, cold-email sequences, follow-up / reply-triage, meetings booked into the diary.
2. **Presence** — adverts written + posted, website/LinkedIn page + posts, case studies, mailers, market updates.
3. **Source** — mapping, longlists, boolean/x-ray, headhunting passive candidates, MPC + float lists off the bench.
4. **Run the desk** — screening prep, CVs formatted to spec, scheduling, references, compliance chasing, ATS hygiene, pipeline reporting.

## Packaging — a half, or the whole desk
- **Win The Work** (pillars 1+2) — outreach + presence; BD that runs without the owner.
- **Fill The Work** (pillars 3+4) — source + run-the-desk; delivery off the owner's plate.
- **Recruit Assist (both)** — one assistant across the full desk, or two specialists (one per side).

## Pricing — one price, one unit, no tiers
- **$1,500 / month** — one trained assistant (~40 hrs/wk), on either half (or split across both).
- **$3,000 / month** — two assistants (one on Win The Work, one on Fill The Work).
- Every engagement includes: **training, our systems + campaign stack, management (by us), replacement if the fit's wrong, and 90-day terms.** Nothing else to buy.
- Positioning math: $1,500/mo = $18k/yr, vs ~$70k all-in for a $55k-salary resourcer (employer taxes, pension, holiday, desk, software) — before ~3 months of ramp.

## Positioning
NOT a VA (a generalist who arrives with no context and becomes your job to manage). It's **"the resourcer you can't quite justify yet"** — trained on recruitment before day one, managed by us, systems included, zero employment risk. Live work inside 2 weeks — no job spec, no interviews, no ramp.
